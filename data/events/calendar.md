# Annual Events Calendar

## TechFest
The annual technical festival, 'TechNova', is scheduled for October 12-14. Events include hackathons, robotics competitions, and coding challenges. Registration is open to all years.

## Cultural Fest
The cultural fest, 'Symphony', will be held from March 5-7. It features inter-college dance, music, and drama competitions. Auditions for the college team start in January.
