# Academics Policies

## Attendance Rules
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all registered courses. Medical leaves can account for up to 10% of attendance waiver if submitted within 5 days of the absence. Failure to meet the 75% requirement will result in an 'F' grade for the course.

## Grading Policy
The college follows a 10-point grading system:
- A: 90-100 (10 points)
- B: 80-89 (9 points)
- C: 70-79 (8 points)
- D: 60-69 (7 points)
- F: <60 (0 points, fail)
A CGPA of 5.5 is required to graduate.
