# Stanford Global Campus — Student Life & Traditions Calendar

## Cultural Fest — Frost Music & Arts Festival
The premier annual cultural festival of Stanford Global Campus is the 'Frost Music & Arts Festival', hosted from May 14-16, 2027 at the historic Frost Amphitheater. Frost is Stanford's signature multi-genre celebration uniting world-class headliner concerts, collegiate musical ensembles, experimental digital media installations, student film screenings, and open-air artisan pavilions. Early-bird student wristbands and visual arts exhibition submissions open in February through Stanford Concert Network (SCN).

## Tech Fest & Hackathon — Stanford TreeHacks 2027
Stanford's flagship collegiate hackathon, 'TreeHacks 2027', takes place February 19-21, 2027 at the Huang Engineering Center and Arrillaga Alumni Center. Over 1,500 builders from across the globe build groundbreaking solutions across Generative AI, Digital Health, and Climate & Sustainability verticals, competing for $120,000+ in seed grants and venture mentorship.

## Traditions & Campus Rally — Gaieties & Big Game Week
The annual fall campus rally and musical revue, 'Gaieties', runs November 19-21, 2026 at Memorial Auditorium. It brings together over 3,000 undergraduate and graduate students in satirical theater, musical parodies, and the historic Big Game bonfire traditions.
