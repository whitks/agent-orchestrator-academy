# Stanford Global Campus — Office of the University Registrar Bulletin

## Attendance & Course Participation
Stanford Global Campus expects consistent academic engagement across all enrolled units. In seminar and laboratory courses, students are required to maintain at least 80% attendance and active discussion participation. Unexcused absences exceeding two sessions in a single academic quarter trigger an Academic Progress Warning. Course withdrawal without academic penalty is permitted through the end of Week 8 with instructor approval.

## Grading Policy & Academic Standing
Stanford Global Campus operates on a 4.3 letter grade point scale:
- A+ (4.3), A (4.0), A- (3.7) — Excellent performance
- B+ (3.3), B (3.0), B- (2.7) — Good performance
- C+ (2.3), C (2.0), C- (1.7) — Satisfactory performance
- D (1.0), NP/NC (0.0) — Unsatisfactory / No Credit
Undergraduate candidates must maintain a minimum cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of 2.0 to remain in good academic standing and qualify for graduation.
