# Stanford Global Campus — Stanford BEAM Career Center Guidelines

## Career Recruitment & On-Campus Interviewing (OCI)
To participate in campus recruitment drives and On-Campus Interviewing (OCI) organized by Stanford BEAM Career Center:
- Students must maintain an active Handshake profile with verified degree status.
- Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.2 is required for premier tech and quantitative finance recruiting tracks.
- Zero active university judicial or honor code holds.
- Must have completed at least one summer technical internship or faculty research fellowship.

## Key Recruitment Cycles
Cardinal Fall Career Fair takes place October 8-10, 2026 at Canfield Court, followed by Winter Technology & Biotech interviews beginning the third week of January.
