# Stanford Global Campus — Residential & Dining Enterprises (R&DE)

## Undergraduate Housing & Facility Access
All undergraduate dormitories and residential houses are managed by Stanford R&DE. Exterior dormitory entrance doors automatically switch to secure electronic Stanford ID badge access at 11:30 PM nightly. Quiet hours are enforced across all residences from 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM on weekdays. Late visitors must be registered with Resident Assistants.

## Library & Study Commons
Green Library and the Bing Wing study lounges offer 24/7 access during midterms and final examination periods with student ID swipe. Regular hours are 8:00 AM to midnight daily. Digital reservations for group collaboration media suites can be booked up to 7 days in advance.
