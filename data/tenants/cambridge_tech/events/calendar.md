# Cambridge Institute of Science — Term Card & Union Societies

## Cultural Fest — May Week Bumps & The Cambridge Union Gala
The chief celebratory cultural festival of Cambridge Institute of Science is 'May Week Bumps & The Cambridge Union Gala', taking place from June 15-18, 2027 across the River Cam and the Trinity and St John's college lawns. The festival pairs historic eight-oared rowing bumps regattas with world-famous white-tie garden parties, midnight orchestral performances, comedy revues by Footlights alumni, and illuminated river punting processions. Wristband ballots for student members open in the second week of Easter Term.

## Tech Fest — Silicon Fen Innovation Summit
The flagship technology conference, 'Silicon Fen Innovation Summit', is held April 22-24, 2027 at the West Cambridge High Performance Computing & Tech Hub. The summit brings together student deep-tech founders, Cambridge venture partners, and AI/Quantum researchers, offering £100,000 in translational proof-of-concept awards.

## Debating & Discourse — Michaelmas Union Open
The premier British Parliamentary inter-collegiate debate tournament, 'Michaelmas Union Open', convenes November 12-14, 2026 at the historic Cambridge Union Debating Chamber on Bridge Street.
