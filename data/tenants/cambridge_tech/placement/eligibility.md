# Cambridge Institute of Science — University Careers Service Guidelines

## Career Recruitment & CAMdata Portal
Undergraduate and postgraduate students seeking internships and milkround graduate positions through the Cambridge University Careers Service:
- Must have achieved or be formally predicted a First Class Honours or Upper Second Class Honours (2:1) in preliminary Tripos examinations.
- Must provide two academic referee endorsements from College Directors of Studies.
- Must possess an active verified profile on the University Careers Service CAMdata portal.

## Cambridge STEM & Finance Fair
The major Michaelmas term recruitment event, the Cambridge University STEM & Finance Fair, convenes October 20-22, 2026 at the University Centre and Student Union on Granta Place.
