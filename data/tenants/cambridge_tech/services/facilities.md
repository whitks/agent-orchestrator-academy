# Cambridge Institute of Science — Collegiate Estates & Student Lodgings

## Porter's Lodge & College Gate Curfew
Junior members residing in college courts must observe college gate regulations. Outer college gates close at 11:00 PM nightly. Late entry between 11:00 PM and 6:00 AM requires reporting to the Head Porter on duty at the Porters' Lodge with University Card authentication. Quiet hours are observed from 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM across all residential staircases.

## Libraries & Dining Buttery
The Cambridge University Library (The UL) and College Libraries operate 8:30 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, extending to 24-hour reading rooms during Easter Term examination preparation. The College Buttery serves subsidized breakfast, lunch, and formal hall dinners daily between 7:30 AM and 9:00 PM.
