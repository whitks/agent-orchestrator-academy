# Cambridge Institute of Science — Statutes & Board of Examinations Ordinances

## Keeping Terms & Academic Attendance
Under the University Statutes, all resident junior members must 'keep terms' by residing within the university precincts (3 miles from Great St Mary’s Church) for at least 59 nights per Michaelmas and Lent Term, and 52 nights per Easter Term. Students must attend a minimum of 85% of all scheduled Supervisions, laboratory practicals, and Tripos discussion lectures. An unexcused absence from two consecutive Supervisions is reported to the Senior Tutor and may lead to a suspension of Examination entry eligibility.

## Tripos Examination & Degree Honours
Degrees are awarded according to historical Tripos honours classifications:
- First Class Honours (Starred First / First): 70% and above
- Upper Second Class Honours (2:1): 60% – 69%
- Lower Second Class Honours (2:2): 50% – 59%
- Third Class Honours: 40% – 49%
Candidates failing to attain at least Third Class honours in Part II/Part III Tripos examinations are not eligible for the Bachelor of Arts (Honours) or Master of Engineering (MEng) degrees.
