# Apex Institute of Technology — Campus Operations & Housing

## Hostel Curfew & Discipline
Curfew hours for all undergraduate residential hostels (Block A through F) is strictly 10:30 PM. Gate entries after 10:30 PM will be marked as late violations in the digital biometric register. Amassing three late violations in a semester leads to an automatic parental notification and disciplinary hearing. Outside food delivery is permitted at main security gates until 11:00 PM.

## Central Library
Apex Central Library provides 24/7 access during semester examination cycles, and 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM on regular instructional days. Regular undergraduate borrowing limit is 5 books for a tenure of 14 days.
