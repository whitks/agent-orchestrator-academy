# Apex Institute of Technology — Academic Regulations

## Attendance Rules
Students at Apex Institute of Technology must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance across all registered lectures and practical courses. A medical leave waiver covering up to 10% of attendance is granted only if genuine medical documentation is submitted to the Dean of Academic Affairs within 5 working days of the absence. Falling below the 75% requirement without an approved medical waiver results in an automatic 'F' (Fail) grade for the affected course.

## Grading Policy
Apex Institute of Technology follows a standard 10-point cumulative grading scale:
- A: 90-100% (10 Grade Points)
- B: 80-89% (9 Grade Points)
- C: 70-79% (8 Grade Points)
- D: 60-69% (7 Grade Points)
- F: Below 60% (0 Grade Points, Fail)
A cumulative GPA (CGPA) of at least 5.5 is required for the award of undergraduate degrees.
