# Apex Institute of Technology — Campus Events & Festivals

## Cultural Fest — Symphony
The annual flagship cultural fest of Apex Institute of Technology, 'Symphony', will be held from March 5-7, 2027 at the Main Campus Auditorium. Symphony is the university's largest annual cultural extravaganza, featuring inter-college western and eastern battle of the bands, classical fusion dance, street play (nukkad natak) championships, fashion showcase, and celebrity concert nights. Team auditions and student organizing committee registrations begin in early January.

## Tech Fest — TechNova 2026
Apex's annual technical festival, 'TechNova 2026', takes place from October 12-14, 2026 across the Science and Computing blocks. Major events include a 24-hour National Hackathon, AI Bot Wars, Autonomous Drone Racing, and Open Innovation Project Expo. Cash prizes exceed Rs. 5,00,000.

## Sports Meet — Apex Spardha
The annual inter-departmental sports tournament, 'Apex Spardha', is held February 10-12, 2027 at the University Sports Complex, featuring cricket, football, basketball, and track events.
