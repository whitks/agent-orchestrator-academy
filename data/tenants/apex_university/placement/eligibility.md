# Apex Institute of Technology — Career Development Cell Placement Guidelines

## Eligibility Criteria
To participate in campus recruitment drives conducted by the Training & Placement (T&P) Cell:
- Students must have zero (0) active backlogs at the time of drive registration.
- Minimum CGPA of 7.0 is required for Tier 1 premium recruitment partners (CTC >= 12 LPA).
- Minimum CGPA of 6.0 is required for Tier 2 and Tier 3 mass recruitment partners (CTC 4 - 10 LPA).
- Students must have completed at least one 8-week accredited industry internship.

## Registration Process
Eligible students must register on the Apex T&P Portal by August 15th with verified resumes.
