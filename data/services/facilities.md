# Campus Services

## Hostel Rules
In-times for all hostels is 10:30 PM. Students arriving after 10:30 PM will be marked late. Three late marks in a month will lead to a disciplinary hearing. Outside food delivery is allowed only until 11:00 PM.

## Library Rules
The central library is open 24/7. Students can borrow up to 5 books for a period of 14 days. Late fees are calculated at Rs. 10 per day. Silence must be maintained in the reading rooms.
