# Placement Guidelines

## Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for campus placements, students must meet the following criteria:
- No active backlogs at the time of registration.
- A minimum CGPA of 7.0 for Tier 1 companies.
- A minimum CGPA of 6.0 for Tier 2 and Tier 3 companies.
- Must have completed at least one 8-week internship.

## Registration Process
Students must register on the placement portal by August 15th. Resume verification will be conducted by the placement cell by August 25th.
